The protocol

Mechanics made visible.

FairDot does not promise stable prices, recovery of quote cost basis, or profit. It gives holders a clearer rule set for positions, checkpoints, and larger exits.

01

Per-lot accounting

Entry is the quote actually paid divided by tracked tokens received. Transfers carry or split basis and timers.

5×

Qualified protection

A 24-hour quote VWAP can qualify a checkpoint and begin the ten-day release design.

↗

Large sale requests can become one, three, or ten fixed installments through the canonical pool.
