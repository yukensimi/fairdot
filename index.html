Why FairDot?

A protocol, not just a token. Any memecoin can integrate FairDot protection.

🚀

Factory-Based Launches

Create a token and canonical pool through a shared Factory. A launch requires a native-quote seed and transaction gas.

🔒

Principal Accounting

FairDot tracks cost basis per lot. Withdrawal eligibility still depends on the rules, pool liquidity, market price, and execution.

📈

Gradual Profit Release

After minimum-volume qualification, a holder checkpoint at 5× entry starts a 10%-per-day release over 10 days.

🐋

Large-Sale Schedule

Qualifying requests are split into installments based on estimated quote output relative to the pool reserve. This is not a guarantee against repeated small sales.

🔗

Lot History on Transfer

Supported wallet-to-wallet transfers carry or split the associated entry basis and timer instead of resetting them.

🛡️

Clear Boundaries

Minting is disabled after launch, but FairDot does not lock liquidity or prevent rug pulls, OTC trades, or market losses.

How It Works

Simple. Fair. Transparent.

1

Launch or Join

Deploy your memecoin with FairDot protection, or browse and trade existing protected tokens on the platform.

2

Buy & Hold

Your weighted-average entry price is recorded on-chain. Every buy updates your position transparently.

3

Qualified 5× Checkpoint

After minimum 24-hour quote volume is met, a holder checkpoint can start the per-lot profit-release schedule.

4

Sell Through the Canonical Pool

Eligibility, stale-market limits, pool liquidity, price impact, and the large-sale schedule can affect whether and when a sale executes.
