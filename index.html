The protocol

Rules in plain sight.

FairDot does not guarantee stable prices, cost-basis recovery, liquidity, or profit. It provides a visible rule set for lots, checkpoints, and large exits.

01

Per-lot accounting

Entry is quote actually paid divided by tracked tokens received. Transfers carry or split basis and timers.

5×

Qualified protection

Volume-qualified VWAP can trigger a five-times checkpoint and a ten-day linear release design.
