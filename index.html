The protocol

Mechanics made visible.

FairDot does not promise stable prices, recovery of quote cost basis, or profit. It gives holders a clearer rule set for how positions, checkpoints, and larger exits are handled.

01

Per-lot accounting

Entry is the quote actually paid divided by tracked tokens received. Same-day buys can merge only into the latest open lot. Transfers carry or split basis and timers.

5×

Qualified protection

A 24-hour quote VWAP must meet the configured volume threshold before a 5× lot-entry checkpoint can begin the ten-day, 10% per-day release design.

↗

Staggered exits

Large sale requests are evaluated against quote reserve. They can become one, three, or ten fixed installments, with later holder-called transactions.

Protocol rules

These are eligibility mechanics, not a guarantee of liquidity, execution, quote recovery, or profit.

VWAP

Volume-qualified protection

Protection needs the maximum of configured percentage × initial quote seed and configured absolute quote volume. Pool observations are not a manipulation-proof oracle.

FIFO

Lots and transfers

Sales use oldest eligible lots first. Same-day open buys may merge; checkpointed or transferred lots remain distinct.

7d

Stale emergency

After more than seven days without a trade, stale mode checkpoints open lots against the last valid price and limits principal withdrawals.
